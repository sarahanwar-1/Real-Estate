    Questions we hear a lot

    Not here? Call (555) 010-0172 and we will answer.

    How much do I need for a down payment?

    Requirements depend on your country and the price of the home. Many buyers put down between 5 and 20 percent. A larger down payment lowers your monthly payment and can remove mortgage insurance. Use the calculator on this page to compare, and ask your lender for the exact rules.

    Do I pay anything to tour homes?

    No. Tours are free, in person or by video. There is no obligation to make an offer.

    How is my home's value estimated?

    The quick estimate on this page uses average prices per square foot in your neighbourhood and the condition you choose. It is a starting point, not an appraisal. For a firm price range, an agent visits the home and compares recent nearby sales.

    How long does it take to sell a home?

    It depends on the price, the condition and the season. Homes priced close to recent nearby sales usually attract offers soonest. Your agent will give you a realistic timeline after seeing the home.

    Can I rent before I buy?

    Yes. We list homes for rent in the same neighbourhoods, and our rental agent can help you find a place while you shop for a home to buy.

    What does it cost to list with Fernhill Realty?

    Fees are agreed in writing before you list, and we explain everything that is included. Ask for a free valuation and we will walk you through the costs.
